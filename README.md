## English

This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected when you use **Tap Out: Arrow Puzzle** (the "App") on Android, how it is used, and the choices you have.

We do not ask you to create an account. We do not collect your name, email address, phone number or payment details ourselves.

### 1. Information stored on your device
Your game progress is saved locally on your device and never leaves it unless you sign in to Google Play Games. This includes:
- levels and stars
- coins, hints and undos
- daily challenge history
- purchased themes
- settings such as sound, music, language and theme

Uninstalling the App deletes this data.

### 2. Google Play Games Services (optional)
If you choose to sign in with Google Play Games, the App uses:
- your Play Games player ID, display name and avatar, to show your profile
- **Saved Games (cloud save)**, to store your progress (level, coins, stars, hints, solved daily challenges and purchased themes) so you can restore it on another device
- leaderboards and achievements

This data is processed by Google under the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). You can play the whole game without signing in.

### 3. Advertising (Google AdMob)
The App shows ads through Google AdMob: a banner, full-screen ads every 10 levels, and optional rewarded videos.

AdMob may collect and use, for ad delivery, measurement, fraud prevention and (where you consent) personalised ads:
- your device's advertising ID
- IP address
- device and app information
- ad interactions

- **Consent:** in the European Economic Area, the UK, Switzerland and certain US states, a consent form from Google's User Messaging Platform is shown before ads are personalised. You can change your choice at any time in **Settings → Privacy options**.
- **Ad personalisation:** you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings (**Settings → Google → Ads** or **Settings → Privacy → Ads**).
- Buying **Remove ads** turns off banner and full-screen ads.

More information: [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

### 4. Analytics and crash reports (Google Firebase)
We use **Firebase Analytics** to understand how the game is played and **Firebase Crashlytics** to find and fix crashes.

**What is collected**
- *Analytics:*
  - app usage events, for example level started or finished, number of mistakes and time, hints used, rewarded ads watched, Shop visits and purchases
  - an app instance ID
  - device model and OS version
  - app version
  - language
  - approximate location (country/region, derived from IP address)
- *Crashlytics:*
  - crash stack traces
  - device state at the time of the crash
  - a crash installation ID
  - the level being played

**What is not collected:** this data is not linked to your name or email and does not include your Play Games profile.

**Why:** improving game balance (for example, levels that are too hard), fixing bugs, and understanding which features are used.

**Retention:** Google retains Analytics data for up to 14 months and Crashlytics data for up to 90 days.

**Consent:** where required, analytics follows the consent you gave in the consent form.

See the [Firebase privacy information](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy).

### 5. In-app purchases (Google Play Billing)
The **Remove ads** purchase is processed entirely by Google Play. We never see your payment card or billing details. The App receives only a purchase confirmation, which it uses to unlock the feature and to let you **Restore purchases**.

Coins, hint packs and themes are bought with in-game coins only; no real money is involved.

### 6. How we use information
We use the information above only to:
- run the game, save and sync your progress
- show ads and offer optional rewarded ads
- process purchases
- analyse usage and fix crashes

We do not sell your personal information.

### 7. Sharing
Information is shared only with the service providers listed above (Google Play Games Services, Google AdMob, Google Firebase and Google Play Billing), who act under their own privacy policies. We may also disclose information if required by law.

### 8. Your choices and data deletion
- **Play without an account:** signing in is optional.
- **Ads:** change your consent in **Settings → Privacy options**, reset your advertising ID in Android settings, or buy **Remove ads**.
- **Local data:** uninstall the App or clear its storage in Android settings.
- **Cloud save:** delete the App's saved game data from your Google Play Games settings (**Play Games app → Settings → Delete Play Games account & data**, or by game).
- **Analytics and crash data:** contact us at the address above to request deletion. We will ask Google to delete the data linked to your app instance.

### 9. Children
The App is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information, contact us and we will delete it.

### 10. Security
Data sent to Google services is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Local data stays in the App's private storage on your device.

### 11. Changes to this policy
We may update this policy from time to time. The new version will be posted at this link with a new effective date. Significant changes will also be announced in the App's store listing.

### 12. Contact
Questions or requests: joviulhag@gmail.com

Anda tidak perlu membuat akun. Kami sendiri tidak mengumpulkan nama, alamat email, nomor telepon, atau data pembayaran Anda.

### 1. Data yang disimpan di perangkat Anda
Progres permainan disimpan secara lokal di perangkat dan tidak keluar dari perangkat kecuali Anda masuk ke Google Play Games. Data ini meliputi:
- level dan bintang
- koin, hint, dan undo

Jika Anda memilih masuk dengan Google Play Games, Aplikasi menggunakan:

Data ini diproses oleh Google sesuai [Kebijakan Privasi Google](https://policies.google.com/privacy?hl=id). Seluruh permainan bisa dimainkan tanpa masuk.

### 3. Iklan (Google AdMob)
Aplikasi menampilkan iklan melalui Google AdMob: banner, iklan layar penuh setiap 10 level, dan video berhadiah yang opsional.

AdMob dapat mengumpulkan dan menggunakan data berikut untuk menayangkan dan mengukur iklan, mencegah penipuan, serta (jika Anda menyetujui) menampilkan iklan yang dipersonalisasi:
- ID iklan perangkat
- alamat IP
- informasi perangkat dan aplikasi
- interaksi dengan iklan

- **Persetujuan:** di Wilayah Ekonomi Eropa, Inggris, Swiss, dan beberapa negara bagian AS, formulir persetujuan dari User Messaging Platform Google ditampilkan sebelum iklan dipersonalisasi. Pilihan ini bisa diubah kapan saja di **Pengaturan → Opsi privasi**.
- **Personalisasi iklan:** ID iklan dapat direset atau dihapus di pengaturan Android (**Setelan → Google → Iklan** atau **Setelan → Privasi → Iklan**).
- Membeli **Hapus iklan** menonaktifkan iklan banner dan layar penuh.

Info lebih lanjut: [Cara Google menggunakan informasi dari situs atau aplikasi yang menggunakan layanannya](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites?hl=id).

### 4. Analitik dan laporan crash (Google Firebase)
Kami menggunakan **Firebase Analytics** untuk memahami cara permainan dimainkan dan **Firebase Crashlytics** untuk menemukan dan memperbaiki crash.

**Yang dikumpulkan**
- *Analytics:*
  - aktivitas dalam aplikasi, misalnya level dimulai atau selesai, jumlah kesalahan dan waktu, hint yang dipakai, video berhadiah yang ditonton, kunjungan dan pembelian di Toko
  - ID instance aplikasi
  - model perangkat dan versi OS
  - versi aplikasi
  - bahasa
  - perkiraan lokasi (negara/wilayah, dari alamat IP)
- *Crashlytics:*
  - jejak error saat crash
  - kondisi perangkat saat crash
  - ID instalasi crash
  - level yang sedang dimainkan

**Yang tidak dikumpulkan:** data ini tidak dikaitkan dengan nama atau email Anda dan tidak berisi profil Play Games Anda.

**Tujuan:** menyeimbangkan permainan (misalnya level yang terlalu sulit), memperbaiki bug, dan mengetahui fitur yang dipakai.

**Masa simpan:** Google menyimpan data Analytics hingga 14 bulan dan data Crashlytics hingga 90 hari.

**Persetujuan:** jika diwajibkan, analitik mengikuti persetujuan yang Anda berikan di formulir persetujuan.

Lihat [informasi privasi Firebase](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy).

Pembelian **Hapus iklan** diproses sepenuhnya oleh Google Play. Kami tidak pernah melihat kartu atau data pembayaran Anda. Aplikasi hanya menerima konfirmasi pembelian, yang dipakai untuk membuka fitur tersebut dan untuk **Pulihkan pembelian**.

Koin, paket hint, dan tema dibeli dengan koin dalam game saja, tanpa uang sungguhan.
